# Assets

The world and character in the game are procedural Three.js geometry. There is no map image behind the gameplay.

`dist/assets/pendant.png` is an AI generated concept photograph of a gold sun coin pendant. It is inspiration for the reward experience and is not an Arcade generated product or verified manufacturable quote.

`reference-assets/island.png` and `reference-assets/sprites.png` preserve the generated references from the earlier 2D prototype. They are not used or delivered with the live 3D game.
