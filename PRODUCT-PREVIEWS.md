# CraftKit physical product previews

Created with OpenAI's built-in imagegen tool. Bases are stored in `dist/assets/mockup-pendant.png`, `dist/assets/mockup-scarf.png`, and `dist/assets/mockup-pillow.png`.

These are illustrative presentation scenes. `dist/product-mockup.js` overlays the player's current design using SVG. Manufacturing references and exports remain separate flat artwork; the mockup photograph is never sent as the manufacturing reference. Real Arcade product images take precedence when returned by the live service. Simulated previews remain labelled.

## Final prompt set

### Pendant
Use case: product-mockup. Create a square photorealistic premium jewelry studio photograph used as a dynamic blank product mockup in an app. A small round gold coin pendant hanging from a fine real gold chain, perfectly front facing. Warm off-white matte limestone backdrop, soft directional daylight from upper left, realistic gold edges and reflected highlights, delicate contact shadow. The coin face is perfectly circular, centered precisely at x=50% y=59% of the square image, radius 19% of image width. The flat engraving area is blank unmarked softly brushed gold, no symbols, no text, no gemstones. Thin raised rim. Chain enters from top left and top right and meets realistic bail above coin. Pendant isolated as sole object. Product occupies enough frame to look compelling at thumbnail sizes. No hands, logo, watermark, graphics, collage.

### Scarf
Use case: product-mockup. Square photorealistic premium silk scarf studio photograph, a single blank ivory silk square laid almost flat, photographed exactly overhead on warm off-white matte limestone. Silk square corners positioned at x=15% y=15%, x=85% y=15%, x=85% y=85%, x=15% y=85%; edges horizontal/vertical, subtle handrolled hem. Very gentle shallow diagonal creases and silk sheen across fabric, no deep folds, no rotated shape. Real natural soft directional daylight from upper left, delicate cast shadow, tangible luxurious fabric. Completely blank neutral white printable face, no designs or border decoration. The image will serve as a dynamic print mockup base so exact centered square silhouette matters. No props, no hands, no typography, no logo, no watermark.

### Pillow
Use case: product-mockup. Square photorealistic premium decorative pillow product studio photograph. One plump square blank ivory linen cushion perfectly front facing with minimal perspective, centered on warm off-white limestone seamless background. Cushion outer corners near x=17% y=17%, x=83% y=17%, x=83% y=83%, x=17% y=83%; gently curved edges from fullness, corner seams, fine visible linen weave, shallow creases, natural soft upper-left daylight and soft realistic cast shadow below. Front printable area mostly flat and blank ivory white. No buttons, no piping in a contrasting color. Image serves as dynamic print product mockup base; centered unrotated face essential. No furniture, props, hands, designs, typography, logo, watermark.
